Methodology

This study analyzes the components of the S&P 500 index as of Q1 2026. Data was collected using automated scripts querying reliable financial data sources. Key metrics were calculated as follows:

  • Dividend Yield: The current annualized forward dividend rate divided by the stock price.
  • 5-Year Dividend Growth (CAGR): Calculated by comparing the total dividends paid in the most recent full year to the total dividends paid 5 years prior. Formula: (Ending Value / Beginning Value) ^ (1/5) - 1.
  • Quadrants: Companies were plotted against the median yield and median growth rate of the analyzed group to categorize them into four distinct quadrants.

Companies with no dividend history or insufficient history to calculate a 5-year growth rate were excluded from the quadrant classifications or labeled as "Unknown Growth".
