Methodology & FAQs

How does the Monte Carlo simulation work?
This tool runs 10,000 randomized simulations of your retirement span. For each year, it generates a random market return based on a normal distribution utilizing your expected annual return as the mean, and assuming an 18% standard deviation (roughly the historical volatility of the S&P 500). Withdrawals are adjusted for your specified inflation rate annually.
What is the "4% Rule"?
The 4% rule is a rule of thumb created by William Bengen in 1994. It suggests that retirees can safely withdraw 4% of their initial retirement portfolio value, adjust that withdrawal amount for inflation each year, and have a very high probability of not running out of money over a 30-year period. This tool lets you stress-test that rule under your own assumptions.
What does Success Probability mean?
Success probability is the percentage of the 10,000 simulated paths where your portfolio balance stays above $0 for the entire duration of your specified retirement length.
