How to Use the Rebalancing Calculator

Portfolio rebalancing is the process of realigning the weightings of your portfolio's assets. As markets move, your asset allocation will drift from its target, altering your risk profile.

This tool helps you restore balance. Simply enter your current holdings and your desired target allocation for each asset. The calculator will determine exactly how much to buy or sell to reach your target.

Optimizing New Cash: If you have new money to invest, enter it in the "New Cash to Invest" field. The calculator will preferentially allocate the new cash to underweight assets first, minimizing the need to sell existing assets (which can trigger taxable events in non-registered accounts).

Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I rebalance my portfolio?

Rebalancing forces you to sell high and buy low. By trimming assets that have outgrown their target allocation and adding to assets that have shrunk, you maintain your desired risk level over time.

How often should I rebalance?

Most experts recommend rebalancing either on a set schedule (e.g., annually or semi-annually) or based on tolerance bands (e.g., whenever an asset drifts more than 5% from its target).
