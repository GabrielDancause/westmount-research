Methodology

This study analyzes the market capitalization concentration of the S&P 500 index as of March 2026.

  • Constituent weights sourced from SlickCharts.com, which derives data from the S&P Dow Jones Indices.
  • Financial metrics (sector, P/E ratio, market cap) enriched via Finviz screener for all 501+ constituents.
  • Concentration metrics computed as cumulative weight of top N stocks (top 5, 10, 20, 50).
  • Sector breakdown uses Finviz sector classifications (similar to GICS).
  • Data represents a point-in-time snapshot. Weights change daily as prices move.
  • Some dual-class stocks (e.g., GOOGL/GOOG) appear as separate entries per index methodology.
