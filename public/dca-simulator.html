Methodology

This tool uses a Monte Carlo simulation with Geometric Brownian Motion (GBM) to model stock price movements. We generate 100 random market paths based on your expected annual return and volatility inputs. For each path, we simulate investing the total amount upfront (Lump Sum) versus spreading it out over your chosen timeframe (DCA).

The "Win Rate" indicates how often one strategy ended with a higher final portfolio value than the other. The initial share price is arbitrarily set to $100 for calculating the average cost per share.

FAQ

What is Dollar Cost Averaging (DCA)?
DCA is an investment strategy where you divide your total investment amount across periodic purchases to reduce the impact of volatility on the overall purchase.

Does DCA or Lump Sum perform better?
Historically, because markets tend to rise over time, Lump Sum investing often beats DCA. However, DCA can reduce sequence of returns risk and is often preferred for psychological reasons during volatile periods. This simulator helps visualize those probabilities.
